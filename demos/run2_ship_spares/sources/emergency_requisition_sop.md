# Vessel Ship Spares & Emergency Requisition SOP (SOP-MAR-204)

## 1. Standard Requisition Workflow
1. Chief Engineer verifies current onboard stock against `ship_spares_catalog.csv` and the Planned Maintenance System (PMS).
2. When `stock_qty <= reorder_point`, raise a Standard Purchase Requisition (PR) in the Fleet ERP.
3. Standard orders under **2,500 USD** are approved by the Vessel Technical Superintendent within 48 hours.
4. Orders of **2,500 USD or above** require secondary sign-off from the Fleet Operations Manager.

## 2. Emergency Requisition for CRITICAL Spares
1. A spare marked **CRITICAL** (such as Main Engine or Navigation safety-critical assemblies) qualifies for Emergency Requisition when onboard stock falls below minimum safe reserve or following an active machinery failure.
2. Emergency requisitions require immediate notification to the **Fleet Technical Superintendent** and **24/7 Fleet Duty Desk**.
3. Air-freight / express courier surcharge is pre-authorized up to **500 USD** for CRITICAL spares; surcharges above 500 USD require Fleet Director approval.
4. Target dispatch from approved OEM warehouse is within **24 hours** of emergency PO issuance, consigned to the port agent at the vessel's next port of call.

## 3. Quality & Traceability
All CRITICAL spares must ship with an OEM Certificate of Conformity (CoC) and Class Society certificate (DNV / Lloyd's Register) where applicable. Non-OEM substitutes are prohibited on Main Engine fuel and steering systems without written Class dispensation.
