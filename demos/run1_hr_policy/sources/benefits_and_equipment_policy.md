# Acme Corp Employee Benefits & Equipment Stipend Policy (2026 Edition)

## 1. Home Office & Equipment Stipend
Full-time employees are eligible for a one-time **1,000 USD home-office setup stipend** after completing 30 days of employment.
Eligible purchases include ergonomic chairs, standing desks, external monitors, keyboards, and noise-canceling headsets.
Receipts must be submitted in Expensify within **45 days** of purchase under cost center `IT-EQUIP-2026`.

## 2. Learning & Development (L&D) Budget
Each full-time employee receives an annual **1,500 USD Learning & Development (L&D) allowance** for job-relevant conferences, certifications, online courses, and technical books.
Unused L&D balances do **not** roll over to the following calendar year and expire on December 15.
Training requests above **500 USD** require advance written approval from the employee's direct manager before registration.

## 3. Health, Wellness & Commuter Benefits
Acme Corp provides a monthly **100 USD wellness reimbursement** covering gym memberships, fitness classes, or mental health apps.
Hybrid and on-site staff qualify for a pre-tax **150 USD monthly commuter benefit** for public transit or approved office parking.

## 4. Equipment Return & Offboarding
Company-issued laptops and security keys remain Acme Corp property and must be returned within **5 business days** of the employee's final working day.
Items purchased with the 1,000 USD home-office stipend become employee-owned after **12 months** of continuous employment.
