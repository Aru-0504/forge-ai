# Acme Corp HR Policy Handbook (2026 Edition)

## 1. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Holidays
Full-time employees accrue **20 days** of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year (1.67 days per month).
Up to **5 unused PTO days** may be carried over into the next calendar year; any balance above 5 days expires on December 31 unless required otherwise by local law.
Requests for 3 or more consecutive PTO days should be submitted in Workday at least **10 business days** in advance for manager approval.
Acme Corp observes **11 paid public holidays** each year.

## 2. Parental Leave
Eligible full-time employees with at least 6 months of tenure receive **16 weeks** of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
Parental leave may be taken continuously or split into up to two blocks within 12 months of the qualifying event.
Employees must submit written notice to HR Operations at least **30 calendar days** prior to the anticipated leave start date.

## 3. Remote & Hybrid Work Policy
Engineering, Product, and Operations roles default to a **hybrid schedule**: 3 days per week in-office (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) and 2 days remote.
Employees may work remotely from an approved domestic location for up to **20 business days per calendar year** with manager sign-off.
International remote work requires written pre-approval from Legal and Tax Compliance at least 21 days in advance due to permanent establishment and payroll withholding rules.

## 4. Travel & Expense Reimbursement
- **Meal per diem (domestic)**: Up to **75 USD per day** with itemized receipts for any single meal exceeding 25 USD.
- **Meal per diem (international)**: Up to **100 USD per day**.
- **Lodging**: Standard business-class hotel up to **250 USD per night** in Tier-1 cities (180 USD in Tier-2 cities).
- **Expense submission deadline**: Expense reports must be filed within **30 days** of trip completion; late reports require VP Finance approval.

## 5. Escalation & Exceptions
Questions not explicitly covered in this handbook, medical accommodation requests, or policy exception requests must be escalated to **HR Operations** (ticket queue: HR-OPS) within 2 business days.
